What We Offer

Our Services

Beyond product supply, OMG Traders supports you with complete export services – from sourcing to container loading – so that importing from India becomes smooth and reliable.

🌐

Global Export & Import

We coordinate with buyers and shipping agents to move agro products from India to your destination port.

📦

Bulk Agro Supply

Supply for traders, wholesalers and supermarkets that need container loads of coconuts, spices or pulses.

🏷️

Private Label

Options for customised packing with your brand name, logo and design (bags / boxes as per discussion).

🚛

Container Logistics

Coordinating loading, stuffing and documentation for containers at the designated port.

📑

Documentation Support

Help with commercial invoice, packing list and other export paperwork required by your country.

🤝

Long‑Term Partnership

Our goal is to become your regular, reliable Indian sourcing partner for agro products.

How It Works

1. You share your product list, quantity and destination port.
2. We revert with prices, packing details and shipment terms.
3. Once confirmed, we arrange sourcing, packing and container loading.
4. All documents are shared for your bank and customs clearance.
