Who We Are

About OMG Traders

OMG Traders is a growing export company from India, focused on delivering clean, quality‑checked agro products to international buyers with complete professionalism and transparency.

Based in Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu, we work closely with farmers, local markets and processing units to source authentic products at the right quality and price. Every shipment is packed as per export requirements and inspected carefully before dispatch.

Our aim is to become a dependable sourcing partner for importers looking for Indian coconuts, spices, pulses, grains and vegetables – with a focus on long‑term business relationships instead of one‑time orders.
